Qiskit Fall Fest GDL 2026

October 28 – 29, 2026 Guadalajara, México

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global series of quantum computing events organized by communities around the world in partnership with IBM Quantum. The Guadalajara edition brought together students, developers, and quantum enthusiasts for two days of talks, hands-on workshops, and quantum labs — right here in Mexico's tech capital. All event content was delivered in Spanish.
