A global quantum computing celebration

Qiskit Fall Fest GDL 2026

October 28 – 30, 2026 Guadalajara, México

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global series of quantum computing events organized by communities around the world in partnership with IBM Quantum. The Guadalajara edition brings together students, developers, and quantum enthusiasts for three days of talks, hands-on workshops, and a quantum hackathon — right here in Mexico's tech capital.

Learn & Build

Quantum Enigmas

Audience: All learners
Languages: English, French
Duration: 8 hours

This credential earner has a foundational understanding of quantum computing, including principles such as quantum superposition, entanglement, and measurement. The individual has considered challenges using the IBM Quantum Composer to map problems onto quantum circuits and explored quantum logic gates. The earner can differentiate between classical and quantum computing, explain quantum state measurement, and apply concepts through interactive problem-solving exercises.

Explore Emerging Tech

Audience: All learners
Languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Korean, Polish, Italian, Turkish, Traditional Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese
Duration: 7+ hours

This credential earner has knowledge of emerging technologies that power today's jobs: data, artificial intelligence (AI), cloud computing, cybersecurity, and quantum computing. The individual knows foundational concepts, terminology, and how technologies are applied to solve problems in organizations and business. The earner can use this knowledge to explore careers in tech.
